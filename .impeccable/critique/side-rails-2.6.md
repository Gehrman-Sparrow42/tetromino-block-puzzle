# Side-rail play screen — 2.6.0

The user's phone screenshot exposed two interacting layout problems: the horizontal HUD/preview rows consumed 92px above the board, and legacy Floating bypassed control-obstacle clearance. The repair removes those rows, starts the centered board at the safe top + 4px, places Hold/statistics on the left, and places Pause/five Next previews on the right. Portrait controls remain below the board; full-width landscape places controls in clear side space.

Argon Medium completed an independent read-only review of the source and 17 renders. It confirmed the direction and identified custom placements hiding Pause/rail content and short-screen clipping. Rail placeholders now make occupied regions visible in the editor and Settings preview. Background rails do not capture touches; Hold and Pause retain their targets.

The review also flagged inconsistent label casing and stale documentation, both corrected.
